Frequency

A personal macOS app for one person's own messages and email.

Frequency is a private, native Mac app that brings my own email accounts and chats into one window. It is used only by its developer and is not offered to the public.

How it works

  • It runs entirely on my Mac. There is no Frequency server.
  • It connects to Gmail through Google's official Gmail API, using Google's own sign-in page.
  • Mail is stored on the Mac in an encrypted database.
