Privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026

Frequency is a personal macOS app used only by its developer to read and send their own email. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data the app accesses

With the account owner's permission, given on Google's sign-in page, Frequency uses the Gmail API to read, label, archive and send email in that owner's Gmail accounts. If instant delivery is turned on, it also uses Google Cloud Pub/Sub, within the owner's own Google Cloud project, to receive "new mail" notifications.

Where data goes

  • Email data is downloaded directly from Google to the owner's Mac and stored there in an encrypted (AES-256) database. The key is kept in the macOS Keychain on that Mac.
  • Frequency has no servers. No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • No data is sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Frequency's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Removing an account in Frequency deletes its stored mail from the Mac. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
